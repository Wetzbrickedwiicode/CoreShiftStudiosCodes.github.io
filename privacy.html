Privacy Policy for CoreshiftStudiosCodeChecker

Last Updated: September 2026

CoreshiftStudiosCodeChecker respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information.

1. Information We Collect

We only process data necessary for the functionality of our bot: Discord User IDs and User Tags to log who uses commands like /validate.

2. How We Use Information

We use this data solely to run our promotional code validation feature, prevent abuse, and log actions for staff review.

3. Data Security

We do not store passwords. Data sent to Roblox is managed via secure, encrypted tokens through official APIs.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions or want data deleted, contact us through our official studio channels.
